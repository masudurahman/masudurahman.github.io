I am an economics and finance researcher based in Kraków, Poland. My research sits at the intersection of empirical corporate finance, sustainable finance regulations (EU Taxonomy and CSRD), and Central and Eastern European macroeconomic dynamics.

I specialize in panel econometrics, quasi-experimental causal inference, and quantitative financial modeling using R, Python, and LSEG Workspace.

Education

I completed my Master's degree in Business and Finance Management at Jagiellonian University in Kraków, Poland in July 2025 (GPA: 4.91/5.00), where I defended my thesis on the impact of corporate social responsibility on the financial performance of commercial banks in Poland under the supervision of Dr. Magdalena Kossowska-Lai. Prior to this, I earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Finance from Green University of Bangladesh in 2020 and a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Accounting and Finance from American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB) in 2017.

In a Nutshell: Research Focus

  • ESG & Sustainable Banking

    Studying how bank size, SASB financial materiality, and EU sustainability disclosures shape commercial bank performance.

  • CEE Exchange Rate Dynamics

    Analyzing PLN, CZK, and HUF currency spillovers against USD, EUR, and global volatility (VIX) via Seemingly Unrelated Regressions.

  • Applied Econometrics

    Implementing fixed-effects panel models, PLS-SEM, and staggered Difference-in-Differences estimators in R and Python.

  • Financial Data Engineering

    Automating empirical research workflows across LSEG Workspace, Python, R, and advanced Excel modeling.
